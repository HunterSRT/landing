Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about QMSAP, QC rules, data and accounts

QMSAP (Quality Management System for Analytical Performance) is a web platform for clinical laboratories. It handles daily internal QC, Levey-Jennings charts, Westgard multi-rule evaluation, Sigma metrics and QGI, TEa libraries, method validation tools and audit-ready PDF/Excel reports in one place.

Medical laboratory specialists, technologists, quality officers and lab managers who run QC on chemistry, immunoassay, hematology and other analyzers and need a clear pass / warn / reject picture every day.

Yes. The interface is responsive, so you can review charts, enter results and check Westgard status from a phone, tablet or desktop browser. Nothing needs to be installed.

Yes. Choose from five themes — Default navy, Light, Dark, Glass and Rose. Each user keeps their own theme, so your choice is remembered every time you sign in. You can also restyle the assistant chat box.

1-2s (warning), 1-3s, 2-2s, R-4s, 4-1s, 10-x and 8-x style trend rules are evaluated automatically per level, with colour-coded status so a violation is obvious at a glance.

Sigma = (TEa − |Bias%|) ÷ CV%. QGI = |Bias%| ÷ (1.65 × CV%) and tells you whether the weak point is imprecision (QGI < 0.8), bias (QGI > 1.2) or both. Bias is measured against the factory mean and CV from your lab data.

QMSAP includes reference libraries (CLIA, Rilibäk, RCPA, biological variation and others). Every TEa is editable per analyte, so you can follow your own laboratory policy.

You can exclude individual results from the statistics, or ask the assistant to "exclude abnormal results". It removes only the results that push your lab ±3SD past the factory limits, and tells you exactly what it excluded and why.

Yes. Data auto-saves and syncs to the cloud so you can continue on another device. A local backup protects you against accidental overwrites.

Access is role-based. Administrators create users and decide which devices and tests each user can see or edit, and whether they may open the admin console.

Yes. Print the current test or all tests as a PDF, or export to Excel. The report layout is fully customizable — pick a layout preset, then fine-tune sections, fonts, colours and spacing.

Accounts are created by your laboratory administrator. If your lab is new to QMSAP, use the Contact Us button and we will reach out on WhatsApp to help with onboarding.

Ask your administrator to reset it from the user-management panel. For security, passwords are never shown or e-mailed in plain text.
